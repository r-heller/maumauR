# maumauR: Simulation and Analysis of the DB Mau Mau Card Game

Provides a configurable engine, Monte Carlo simulator, and strategy
framework for the "der kleine ICE" Mau Mau card game, a German
children's variant of Mau Mau with draw-two, skip, and wild cards.
Includes win-rate estimation by seat and player count, pluggable
artificial strategies for comparison, game-length and draw-count
distribution summaries, and an interactive Shiny application for playing
against the simulated opponents.
